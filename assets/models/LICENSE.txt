No dental model is currently bundled with DentalViz.

Before adding a model to this directory, record its source URL, author, exact license,
redistribution permission, and any modifications in this file. STL files contain no reliable
unit metadata; DentalViz therefore labels 1 model unit = 1 mm as an explicit assumption.

The procedural tooth and files under tests/fixtures are project-authored test geometry, not
clinical assets or patient data.
